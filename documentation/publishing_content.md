## Website case study summary

TeleConnect needed a clearer view of who was leaving, why churn occurred, and which active customers should receive retention attention. I normalized a 38-column customer file into five related MySQL tables, built a staging-based loading process, validated customer and revenue data, and created basic and advanced SQL analyses. The analysis found 26.54% churn across all customers, with especially high rates among Month-to-Month customers and customers in their first year. Competitor-related reasons represented 45.00% of churn. I also created reusable customer and segment views and a rule-based list of 442 active, high-value Month-to-Month customers for retention outreach.

## LinkedIn post

I have completed my second data analytics portfolio project: **TeleConnect Customer Churn and Revenue Risk Analysis**.

Using MySQL and MySQL Workbench, I transformed a flat telecom customer dataset into a normalized relational database and analysed 7,043 customer records. The project uses joins, conditional aggregation, CTEs, subqueries, window functions, and reusable SQL views.

Key findings include a 26.54% churn rate across all customers, 45.84% churn among Month-to-Month customers, and 59.87% churn among customers in their first 12 months. Competitor-related reasons accounted for 45.00% of churn. I also developed a transparent rule-based segment of 442 active customers for retention outreach.

The complete repository includes the SQL scripts, database model, validation log, query results, screenshots, findings, recommendations, and project documentation.
